# Spot illustration family

## Asset ID
`SPT-001`–`SPT-006` · six independent Editorial masters.

## Objective
Explain six product principles through compact symbolic scenes that support—not replace—nearby copy.

## Product truth
Hanoman is docs-driven, makes sessions visible, isolates worktrees, keeps humans in control, supports parallel sessions, and preserves knowledge in docs.

## Character tier
Editorial · `MOD-001`; reduced detail, canonical profile, one gesture and one prop per spot.

## Composition
One 1:1 focal action, figure 35–55%, quiet field, and a single motif. Family rhythm is consistent but silhouettes differ.

## Layer map
Shared `bg / environment / character-base / character-gesture / buntut-fx / foreground / text-safe` plus the row-specific layer.

## Responsive outputs
1:1 master, 4:3 docs crop, 4:5 card, 128 px reduced-detail export, and one-color silhouette.

## Alt-text intent
Use each row’s intent plus its factual product object; omit costume inventory. Decorative repetitions receive empty alt.

## Human handoff
Sketch all six as one grid, approve their contrast as a family, then refine individually. AI path uses
`IDENTITY_CORE + TIER_EDITORIAL + COMP_SPOT + the row scene + NEGATIVE_CORE`; human redraw is required.

## Do
Keep each concept distinguishable by action and prop; make the series calm, concise, and truthful.

## Don't
Don't bake labels, logos, code, terminal text, or turn metaphor into an unsupported capability claim.

## Acceptance
Gate 0 maps all six to product docs; Gates 1–2 approve motif/model; Gate 3 approves the family grid;
Gate 4 passes 128 px, one-color, crop, culture, and alt-text checks; Gate 5 packages six editable manifests.
