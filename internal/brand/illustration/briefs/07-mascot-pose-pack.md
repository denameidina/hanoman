Provide a consistent Anoman vocabulary for product, community, and editorial moments without creating a separate character.

## Layer map
Reusable `head-base / torso-costume / legs` plus redrawn `forearm-hand / head-expression / tail / prop / shadow`; avoid rigid puppet joints.

## Responsive outputs
Avatar 32/64 px, sticker 96/256 px, product 160/320/480 px, social 480 px+. Supply transparent SVG/PNG and one-color proofs.

## Alt-text intent
Name the meaningful action only when non-decorative: “Anoman signals that a decision is needed.”

## Human handoff
Review an eight-pose consistency grid at equal optical height, then test each at smallest target. Core drawing is human-first; AI may explore gesture thumbnails only.

## Do
Redraw joints per pose, preserve profile direction, and make expression readable through eye/brow, muzzle, head, hand, and tail.

## Don't
Don't mirror asymmetrical artwork, inflate chibi proportions, use an emoji face, or swap in generic monkey anatomy.

## Acceptance
Gate 2 requires overlay, silhouette, one-color, 32 px, and cultural checks; Gates 3–4 approve the pose grid and expression clarity; Gate 5 delivers reusable parts and eight manifests.
