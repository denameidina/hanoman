# Social and release templates

## Asset ID
`SOC-001`–`SOC-004` · one modular illustration system in four aspect masters.

## Objective
Support releases and community communication while keeping artwork reusable and copy editable.

## Product truth
Release claims, dates, metrics, quotes, and feature proof come from approved copy/product evidence outside the illustration.

## Character tier
Editorial · `MOD-001`; use Mascot only for community reactions, never mix tiers in one frame without art-director approval.

## Composition
Three independent zones: character, product-proof object, and headline-safe field. Logo belongs to layout,
not illustration; keep the brand-mark exclusion area clear and follow its placement guide.

| ID | Ratio | Character zone | Headline/product-proof safe area | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SOC-001` | 1:1 | lower-left 45% | upper/right; center proof | Community update |
| `SOC-002` | 4:5 | lower half | upper third; mid proof | Feature release |
| `SOC-003` | 16:9 | left 40% | right third; center proof | Changelog/header |
| `SOC-004` | 9:16 | lower 45% | top UI-safe band; mid proof | Quote/story |

## Layer map
`bg-light / bg-dark / environment / character-base / character-gesture / proof-object / buntut-fx / foreground / headline-safe / logo-exclusion`.

## Responsive outputs
Art-direct each ratio from shared parts; inheritance preserves eye/gesture/proof relation, not coordinates. Export light field and approved dark-field variant where contrast passes.

## Alt-text intent
Describe the scene plus the factual release point supplied by surrounding copy; quote text remains real text, not part of alt for the artwork.

## Human handoff
Build a four-frame safe-zone board and validate common crop inheritance. AI prompt:
`IDENTITY_CORE + TIER_EDITORIAL + STYLE_EDITORIAL_WAYANG + COMP_SOCIAL + release theme + NEGATIVE_CORE`; redraw final layers.

## Do
Keep master art text-free, leave deliberate proof/headline space, and test logo exclusion on every ratio.

## Don't
Don't bake logo, headline, screenshot, metric, quote, or date; don't force a center crop between ratios.

## Acceptance
Gates 0–3 approve proof and safe-zone board; Gate 4 passes light/dark, crop, contrast, and text-free checks;
Gate 5 delivers four editable masters, export matrix, alt intent, provenance, and manifests.
