# Human and AI workflow / Alur manusia dan AI

AI is an exploration tool, not the authority for cultural truth, product truth, or canonical anatomy.

## Roles and RACI

| Work | Product owner | Art director | Illustrator | AI operator | Cultural reviewer | Accessibility | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product truth | A/R | C | I | I | I | I | I |
| References/model | C | A | R | C | R | I | I |
| Composition/art | C | A | R | R | C | C | I |
| Final access/packaging | I | C | C | I | C | A/R | R |

`A` accountable, `R` responsible, `C` consulted, `I` informed. One named human remains accountable.

## Shared intake

1. Assign catalog ID, owner, surface, audience, product truth, tier, and objective.
2. Build the reference board under [the reference protocol](references.md); record rights and AI-use status.
3. Pass Gate 0 (truth) and Gate 1 (references) before visual generation.

## Human-first path — eight steps

1. Read brief, product proof, model sheet, and accepted references.
2. Produce three composition thumbnails with one focal action.
3. Select at Gate 3; log why alternatives were rejected.
4. Construct canonical silhouette and gesture from the model sheet.
5. Refine costume, motif, semantic color, and layer structure.
6. Draw art-directed responsive crops and write draft alt text.
7. Run cultural, accessibility, and art review at Gate 4; correct findings.
8. Export, package sources/provenance, run QA, and close Gate 5.

## AI-assisted path — eight steps

1. Confirm the brief and only use reference images approved for model input.
2. Assemble versioned prompt blocks; record model/version and job/seed when available.
3. Generate low-cost composition studies, not a final identity master.
4. Reject outputs that violate anatomy, tradition, product truth, or rights; shortlist composition only.
5. At Gate 3, a human selects or rejects; never average incompatible outputs.
6. Illustrator redraws face/profile, hands, costume, tail, tatahan, and product props into editable layers.
7. Human reviewers run Gate 4 and document manual changes plus AI disclosure.
8. Export and close Gate 5 with prompt record, rights, source, QA, and manifest.

## Path decision

| Path | Use when | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|
| Human-first | Model sheet, mascot base, diagram, culturally sensitive close-up | Schedule cannot support canonical redraw |
| AI-assisted | Broad composition ideation with licensed inputs | Output would ship without human reconstruction |
| Hybrid | Hero/lakon/editorial scenes: AI thumbnails + human master | Roles or provenance cannot be recorded |

## Gates 0–5

| Gate | Entry | Exit evidence | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Product truth | Owner + source docs | Approved truth/action | Unsupported capability or unclear owner |
| 1 References | Reference records | Accepted board + rights | Mixed tradition, unknown license, weak claim |
| 2 Character | Approved model draft | Silhouette, tier, cultural tests | Profile/costume/identity drift |
| 3 Composition | ≥3 thumbnails | Selected layout + crop plan | Multiple messages or unsafe crop |
| 4 Artwork | Refined editable master | Art/cultural/access review | Anatomy, fake UI, contrast, or source issue |
| 5 Delivery | Approved artwork | Manifest + export QA | Missing source, disclosure, rights, or output |

## Correction checklist

- Hands: five readable digits when visible; plausible joint direction; no fused objects.
- Eyes: one-eye profile logic; no accidental second eye or frontal emoji construction.
- Costume: supit urang, ulur-ulur, and approved ornament remain coherent.
- Tail: one tail, intentional connection and gesture; no tangles or extra appendages.
- Tatahan: designed rhythm, not noisy pseudo-detail.
- Product props: abstract truthful states; no invented controls or claims.
- Remove baked text, watermark/signature, fake UI, and mixed regional/Asian ornament.

## Iteration, rejection, escalation

Version masters `v01`, `v02`; never overwrite an approved file. Record each rejected direction with
reason (`truth`, `culture`, `identity`, `access`, `rights`, `craft`). Two failed correction rounds on
the same issue return to the prior gate. Product ambiguity escalates to product owner; cultural
ambiguity to reviewer; rights ambiguity means do not use the reference.

## Disclosure and provenance

The manifest names path, people, AI model/version/date, prompt blocks, job/seed when available,
input-reference rights, selected output, manual redraws, and reviewers. “AI-assisted” never implies
deterministic reproduction or transfers accountability away from humans.
