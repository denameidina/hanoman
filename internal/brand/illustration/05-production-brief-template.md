# Production brief template / Templat brief produksi

Salin templat ini untuk setiap master aset. Instruksi dan contoh di bawah menjelaskan standar minimum;
hapus contoh ketika brief aktual sudah lengkap.

## Metadata
Catat ID, judul, owner, versi, tanggal, status, dan surfaces. Contoh: `HRO-001 · Homepage hero · Brand lead · v01 · Draft · web`.

## Objective
Nyatakan satu hasil komunikasi. Contoh: “Show that Hanoman turns documented intent into visible, reviewable work.”

## Audience and action
Sebut audiens dan tindakan setelah melihat. Contoh: “Small engineering teams; inspect a session from the dashboard.”

## Product truth
Tautkan fakta produk yang tidak boleh didramatisasi. Contoh: “Docs guide work; humans approve consequential decisions.”

## Lakon/principle
Pilih satu lakon atau prinsip dan jelaskan pemetaan, bukan klaim kesetaraan. Contoh: “Anoman Duta → carrying a clear mandate and returning evidence.”

## Character tier
Pilih Narrative, Editorial, atau Mascot dan ID model. Contoh: `Narrative · MOD-001`.

## Scene and emotional beat
Tulis satu aksi utama serta rasa masuk/keluar. Contoh: “Focused crossing becomes calm confidence after evidence returns.”

## Locked anchors
Daftar ciri yang tak boleh berubah. Contoh: “White rewanda, one-eye profile, lifted head, supit urang, ulur-ulur, long Buntut tail.”

## Motifs and props
Pilih motif bermakna dan props produk yang faktual. Contoh: “Mandate ring, document folios, session cards; no literal fake UI.”

## Composition
Tentukan rasio, focal action, figure scale, negative space, dan safe area. Contoh: “16:9; figure 45%; left-to-right passage; right third copy-safe.”

## Color/detail
Gunakan semantic roles dan tingkat detail. Contoh: “Bone body, ink contour, brass intent; Narrative tatahan density.”

## Layer map
Tuliskan layer editable. Contoh: `bg / environment / character-base / character-gesture / buntut-fx / foreground / text-safe`.

## Responsive outputs
Daftar crop dan perubahan komposisi, bukan auto-crop semata. Contoh: “16:9 master; art-directed 4:5, 1:1, 9:16; retain eye, hand, tail cue.”

## Alt-text intent
Nyatakan informasi yang harus dipahami tanpa gambar. Contoh: “Anoman carries documented intent through a visible workflow and returns evidence.”

## References and rights
Daftar reference IDs, supported claims, license/status, dan izin AI. Contoh: `REF-SKA-01 · silhouette only · research reference · not uploaded to model`.

## Executor path
Pilih human-first, AI-assisted, atau hybrid serta alasan. Contoh: “Hybrid: AI thumbnails, illustrator redraws canonical anatomy and final vectors.”

## Human handoff
Berikan keputusan komposisi, model sheet, layer, crop, dan titik review. Contoh: “Three thumbnails → Gate 3 → one refined master → Gate 4.”

## AI prompt blocks
Catat block IDs, model/version, reference policy, dan repair plan. Contoh: `IDENTITY_CORE + TIER_NARRATIVE + COMP_HERO + MOTIF_DUTA + NEGATIVE_CORE`.

## Do
Daftar tindakan positif yang dapat dinilai. Contoh: “Keep one focal action and make the human review point visible.”

## Don't
Daftar kegagalan spesifik. Contoh: “Don't bake text/logo, invent product UI, mirror the profile, or mix regional ornament.”

## Acceptance
Ubah objective menjadi pemeriksaan pass/fail. Contoh: “At 320 px, mandate, character, and evidence-return path remain readable.”

## Approval record

| Gate | Owner | Date | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate 0 · Product truth | Product owner | YYYY-MM-DD | Linked product docs |
| Gate 1 · References | Art director + cultural reviewer | YYYY-MM-DD | Accepted reference board |
| Gate 2 · Character | Art director + cultural reviewer | YYYY-MM-DD | Model/silhouette tests |
| Gate 3 · Composition | Product owner + art director | YYYY-MM-DD | Selected thumbnail |
| Gate 4 · Artwork | Art/accessibility reviewers | YYYY-MM-DD | Review export and fixes |
| Gate 5 · Delivery | Delivery reviewer | YYYY-MM-DD | Manifest and QA report |

## Delivery manifest
Link source, exports, checksums/version, prompt record, rights, alt text, QA, and approval. Contoh: `delivery/HRO-001/manifest.md`.
