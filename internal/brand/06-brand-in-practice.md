# Brand in practice / Brand dalam praktik

Contoh di sini menerapkan [personality & voice](03-personality-voice.md) dan
[messaging](04-messaging.md). **Approved** dapat dipakai apa adanya setelah fakta produk diverifikasi.
**Adaptable** boleh diubah mengikuti konteks tanpa mengubah prinsip. **Avoid** menunjukkan arah yang
harus ditolak, bukan copy alternatif.

The examples here apply [personality & voice](03-personality-voice.md) and
[messaging](04-messaging.md). **Approved** copy may be used as written after product facts are
verified. **Adaptable** copy may change with context without changing the principle. **Avoid** shows
the direction to reject, not an alternative.

## 1. Homepage hero

**Context / Konteks:** First contact. Audiens harus memahami kategori sebelum cerita empat lakon.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia**

- Eyebrow: `Open-source control room untuk AI coding agents`
- Headline: `Banyak agent. Satu kendali.`
- Subhead: `Jalankan coding agent lintas project dengan konteks yang benar, session yang terlihat,
  worktree terisolasi, dan kendali yang tetap berada di tangan tim.`
- Primary CTA: `Pasang Hanoman`
- Secondary CTA: `Lihat cara kerjanya`

**English**

- Eyebrow: `The open-source control room for AI coding agents`
- Headline: `Many agents. One control room.`
- Subhead: `Run coding agents across projects with the right context, visible sessions, isolated
  worktrees, and control that stays with your team.`
- Primary CTA: `Install Hanoman`
- Secondary CTA: `See how it works`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** Category, audience value, and proof arrive before mythology. A
dominant Anoman Duta illustration can carry the cultural story beside this literal copy.

**Avoid:** `Bangkitkan pasukan agent terkuat dan taklukkan semua project.` / `Summon the ultimate
agent army and conquer every project.`

## 2. README opening

**Context / Konteks:** Developer sudah berada dekat instalasi dan membutuhkan fungsi yang konkret.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia**

> **Hanoman adalah control room open-source untuk menjalankan coding agent lintas project.** Brief dan
> QA finding bergerak menjadi session interaktif di worktree terisolasi; tim memantau lewat terminal,
> dapat steer atau interupsi, lalu menerima hasil sebagai branch, diff, laporan, dan docs yang dapat
> dilanjutkan.

**English**

> **Hanoman is the open-source control room for running coding agents across projects.** Briefs and
> QA findings become interactive sessions in isolated worktrees; teams monitor them through the
> terminal, steer or interrupt when needed, and receive outcomes as branches, diffs, reports, and
> docs ready to continue.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** It names inputs, mechanism, human control, and outputs before
installation instructions.

**Avoid:** `Hanoman brings the legendary power of the white monkey to your codebase.`

## 3. About / manifesto passage

**Context / Konteks:** Long-form brand surface where mythology may be dominant.  
**Status:** **Adaptable**

**Indonesia**

> Anoman tidak dikenal karena daya yang diam. Ia menerima amanat, menyeberangi batas, bertindak ketika
> tujuan jelas, membawa konteks ketika kepastian tidak cukup, lalu meninggalkan kisah yang hidup lebih
> lama daripada perjalanannya. Hanoman mengambil siklus itu untuk engineering bersama agent: konteks
> ikut bergerak, kerja tetap terlihat, hasil kembali kepada tim, dan pengetahuan menjadi pijakan bagi
> amanat berikutnya.

**English**

> Anoman is not remembered for idle strength. He carries a mandate across boundaries, acts when the
> purpose is clear, brings context when certainty is insufficient, and leaves a story that outlives
> the journey. Hanoman brings that cycle to agent-assisted engineering: context travels, work remains
> visible, outcomes return to the team, and knowledge grounds the next mandate.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** The story maps directly to product principles and does not claim
the brand interpretation is a universal teaching. Link to [the four lakons](02-four-lakons.md) and
[sources](sources.md).

**Avoid:** `Hanoman adalah reinkarnasi digital sang dewa kera untuk zaman AI.` / `Hanoman is the
digital reincarnation of the monkey god for the AI age.`

## 4. First-run onboarding

**Context / Konteks:** User has installed Hanoman and needs the first concrete action.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia**

- Title: `Mulai dari satu project`
- Body: `Tambahkan repository yang sudah ada atau buat project baru. Hanoman akan membantu menyusun
  docs sebelum backlog pertama dijalankan.`
- CTA: `Tambahkan project`
- Hint: `Kamu tetap memilih repository, objective, dan kapan session dibuka.`

**English**

- Title: `Start with one project`
- Body: `Add an existing repository or create a new project. Hanoman will help establish the docs
  before the first backlog runs.`
- CTA: `Add project`
- Hint: `You still choose the repository, objective, and when a session starts.`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** It reduces scope, explains what happens next, and makes control
explicit.

**Avoid:** `Pilih kerajaan pertama untuk memulai lakon.` / `Choose your first kingdom to begin the
story.`

## 5. Empty backlog state

**Context / Konteks:** Product UI; literal action matters more than brand theater.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia**

- Title: `Belum ada backlog`
- Body: `Tulis brief untuk fitur baru atau catat QA finding untuk masalah yang sudah terlihat.`
- CTA 1: `Tulis brief`
- CTA 2: `Catat QA finding`

**English**

- Title: `No backlog yet`
- Body: `Write a brief for a new feature or capture a QA finding for a problem you can already see.`
- CTA 1: `Write brief`
- CTA 2: `Capture QA finding`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** It names the empty object and provides the two valid sources of
work.

**Avoid:** `Kelir menunggu amanat pertamanya.` / `The stage awaits its first mandate.`

## 6. Session-running status

**Context / Konteks:** Operational state; user needs position and progress.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia:** `Session aktif · Execute · 4 dari 7 checklist selesai`  
**English:** `Session active · Execute · 4 of 7 checklist items complete`

Supporting action / Tindakan: `Buka terminal` / `Open terminal`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** State, phase, evidence, and next action fit on one scan line.

**Avoid:** `Anoman sedang membakar penghalang keempat.` / `Anoman is burning through the fourth
obstacle.`

## 7. Recoverable error

**Context / Konteks:** Session cannot start because a prerequisite is absent.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia**

- Title: `Session belum dapat dibuka`
- Body: `tmux tidak ditemukan. Tidak ada worktree atau branch yang dibuat.`
- Recovery: `Pasang tmux, jalankan hanoman doctor, lalu coba lagi.`
- CTA: `Jalankan pemeriksaan lagi`

**English**

- Title: `The session could not start`
- Body: `tmux was not found. No worktree or branch was created.`
- Recovery: `Install tmux, run hanoman doctor, then try again.`
- CTA: `Run checks again`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** It states cause, confirms the absence of side effects, and gives
a deterministic recovery path.

**Avoid:** `Daya Bayu belum menyertai session ini.` / `The wind has not blessed this session.`

## 8. Destructive confirmation

**Context / Konteks:** Deleting an unused branch; consequences must be explicit.  
**Status:** **Approved pattern**

**Indonesia**

- Title: `Hapus branch “feature/example”?`
- Body: `Branch lokal akan dihapus. Commit yang belum tersedia di branch lain dapat sulit dipulihkan.`
- Primary action: `Hapus branch`
- Secondary action: `Batal`

**English**

- Title: `Delete branch “feature/example”?`
- Body: `The local branch will be deleted. Commits unavailable on another branch may be difficult to
  recover.`
- Primary action: `Delete branch`
- Secondary action: `Cancel`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** The object, scope, and consequence are named; the button repeats
the action.

**Avoid:** `Obong branch ini?` / `Set this branch aflame?`

## 9. Release announcement

**Context / Konteks:** Announcing session resume support; one lakon may frame the change.  
**Status:** **Adaptable**

**Indonesia**

- Eyebrow: `Chiranjivi · pekerjaan yang dapat dilanjutkan`
- Headline: `Session kembali ke tempat ia berhenti.`
- Body: `Hanoman kini mempertahankan worktree, branch, fase, dan artefak session saat pekerjaan perlu
  dilanjutkan. Tim tidak harus memulai amanat yang sama dari nol.`
- Proof: `Lihat perubahan dan batas resume di release notes.`

**English**

- Eyebrow: `Chiranjivi · work that can continue`
- Headline: `A session returns to where it stopped.`
- Body: `Hanoman now preserves the worktree, branch, phase, and session artifacts when work needs to
  resume. The team does not have to restart the same mandate from zero.`
- Proof: `See resume behavior and boundaries in the release notes.`

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** Chiranjivi explains continuity, while the body names concrete
product state and the proof link carries detail.

**Avoid:** `Session Hanoman kini hidup abadi.` / `Hanoman sessions are now immortal.`

## 10. Community contribution invitation

**Context / Konteks:** Open-source contribution page.  
**Status:** **Approved**

**Indonesia**

> Hanoman dibangun untuk tim kecil, dan tumbuh melalui pengetahuan yang dibawa pulang komunitas. Bawa
> bug yang dapat direproduksi, keputusan yang dapat dijelaskan, atau perbaikan docs yang membuat agent
> berikutnya bekerja dengan konteks lebih baik. Baca contribution guide, pilih issue, lalu tunjukkan
> bukti perubahanmu.

**English**

> Hanoman is built for small teams and grows through knowledge returned by its community. Bring a
> reproducible bug, an explainable decision, or a docs improvement that gives the next agent better
> context. Read the contribution guide, choose an issue, and show the evidence behind your change.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** It frames contribution as durable knowledge and sets an evidence
standard without gatekeeping.

**Avoid:** `Join the elite monkey army building the future of AI.`

## 12. Illustration brief — Anoman Duta

**Context / Konteks:** Homepage section for `Grounded in context`.  
**Status:** **Adaptable brief**

**Approved direction / Arah approved:** Profil Anoman bergerak melintasi bidang kelir dengan satu
garis dari cincin atau amanat menuju panel docs, lalu garis kembali sebagai marker hasil. Gunakan
bone, ink, dan brass sesuai design system; biarkan screenshot atau diagram objective/spec berada dekat
ilustrasi.

Side-profile Anoman crosses a kelir-like field as one line carries a ring or mandate toward a docs
panel, then returns as an outcome marker. Use the design-system roles for bone, ink, and brass; place
an objective/spec screenshot or diagram nearby.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** The crossing represents context transfer and the return path
represents evidence.

**Avoid:** Anoman flying with random code glyphs and no visible sender, destination, or return.

## 13. Illustration brief — Anoman Obong

**Context / Konteks:** Release or feature story about execution and report-back.  
**Status:** **Adaptable brief**

**Approved direction / Arah approved:** Mulai dari komposisi tenang, lalu satu garis Buntut menjadi
nyala terkendali yang menembus hambatan berbentuk checklist. Gerak berhenti pada branch/diff yang
jelas; tidak ada kota terbakar atau visual kehancuran.

Begin with a calm composition, then let one Buntut line become a controlled flame crossing a
checklist-shaped obstacle. Motion ends on a clear branch/diff; no burning city or destruction imagery.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** It captures transformed force while keeping the product outcome
and guardrail visible.

**Avoid:** Aggressive fire, explosions, angry mascot expressions, or copy celebrating destruction.

## 14. Illustration brief — Gunung Dronagiri

**Context / Konteks:** Docs or case study about uncertain requirements.  
**Status:** **Adaptable brief**

**Approved direction / Arah approved:** Figur Anoman menopang massa gunung yang tersusun dari lapisan
objective, docs, history, diff, dan pertanyaan. Hanya lapisan relevan yang berlabel; seorang operator
di sisi tujuan memegang keputusan akhir.

Anoman supports a mountain mass built from layers of objective, docs, history, diff, and questions.
Only relevant layers are labeled; an operator at the destination holds the final decision.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** The image communicates sufficient context and expertise without
claiming more data is always better.

**Avoid:** An infinitely large data mountain, random files, or a lone agent deciding without an
operator.

## 15. Illustration brief — Chiranjivi

**Context / Konteks:** About page or feature story about docs and session continuation.  
**Status:** **Adaptable brief**

**Approved direction / Arah approved:** Buntut membentuk spiral yang melewati beberapa frame—session,
branch, merge, docs—dan keluar sebagai pijakan bagi figur berikutnya. Setiap putaran berubah sedikit;
spiral tidak menjadi loading loop.

Buntut forms a spiral passing through several frames—session, branch, merge, docs—and exits as
ground for the next figure. Each turn changes slightly; the spiral never becomes a loading loop.

**Why it works / Mengapa bekerja:** Continuity is shown as maintained evolution rather than static
immortality.

**Avoid:** Infinity symbols, eternal loading, or claims that docs never need correction.

## Reuse checklist / Checklist penggunaan ulang

Before adapting an example / Sebelum mengadaptasi contoh:

1. Verify the product fact and current label. / Verifikasi fakta produk dan label terkini.
2. Keep state, action, impact, and control literal in product surfaces. / Jaga keadaan, tindakan,
   dampak, dan kendali tetap literal di produk.
3. Use no more than one lakon as the narrative frame. / Gunakan maksimal satu lakon sebagai kerangka.
4. Check Indonesian and English for equivalent decisions, not identical syntax. / Periksa kesetaraan
   keputusan, bukan kesamaan sintaks.
5. Link cultural claims to [sources](sources.md). / Tautkan klaim budaya ke sources.
6. Test the copy without the illustration or metaphor. / Uji copy tanpa ilustrasi atau metafora.
