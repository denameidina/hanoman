# Character model sheet / Model karakter

## Source versus production

Surakarta references support one visible eye, lifted head, flatter rear shoulder, prominent supit
urang, ulur-ulur, and rewanda classification. Hanoman proportions, color roles, and Buntut link are
production decisions; see [references](references.md).

## Canonical anchors

White rewanda; one-eye profile; slightly lifted head; relatively flat rear shoulder; supit urang;
ulur-ulur; long Buntut-linked tail; agile non-bodybuilder body; intelligent, dignified expression;
bone body, ink structure, brass intent/action.

## Adaptable details

Tatahan/sungging density, minor costume layers, limb elongation, fur marks, texture, and motion may
change by tier without changing silhouette anchors.

## Tier comparison

| Tier | Detail | Proportion | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative | Full costume/gesture | Longest, wayang-led | Hero, lakon |
| Editorial | Interior detail ±half | Moderately compact | Spots, diagram, social |
| Mascot | Essential contour | 3.5–4 head units | UI, stickers |

## Required views

Presentation profile, back silhouette, three-quarter adaptation, neutral/action line, costume
exploded view, and head/eye/mouth/supit urang/ulur-ulur/hand/foot/tail callouts.

## Expressions and tails

EXP-01 neutral; EXP-02 welcome; EXP-03 focused; EXP-04 curious; EXP-05 concerned; EXP-06 firm;
EXP-07 relieved; EXP-08 grateful. Read through eye/brow, muzzle, head, hand, tail.

TAL-01 neutral curve; TAL-02 guide/path; TAL-03 controlled action; TAL-04 continuity spiral.

## Tests and layers

One-color silhouette; 32/64/128 px thumbnail; minimum-size; responsive crop; Narrative–Mascot
overlay; profile/eye; costume-source. Use shared layers plus separate head, forearm/hand, costume
accent, tail.
