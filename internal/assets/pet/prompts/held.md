ROW "held" (8 frames, LOOPS): he is being picked up and held in the air by the viewer, dangling from an invisible grip at the back of his neck/collar — dignified and startled, never slapstick. He stays in profile facing right. This row is an exception to the fixed-feet rule: his feet are OFF the ground and touch nothing. Do NOT draw a hand, a rope, a hook or any other prop — only the character. The head, shoulders and upper chest are the FIXED part: draw them at EXACTLY the same position and size in all 8 frames, as if pinned. The single visible eye is WIDE OPEN throughout — a large round eye with the eyebrow raised high and a small open mouth; his expression does not change between frames. The arms hang loosely down from the shoulders and sway only slightly. The short legs dangle straight down and swing gently left-right like a pendulum: frame 1 centred, frames 2-3 swinging forward (to the right), frame 4 back at centre, frames 5-6 swinging back (to the left), frames 7-8 returning toward centre so that frame 8 leads into frame 1 with a step no larger than the others. The long tail hangs down and sways in the OPPOSITE direction to the legs, its golden tip trailing last. Keep the swing small — no more than about a tenth of his body width in either direction — and make every step the same size, because this row is played on repeat.
