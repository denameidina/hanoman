ROW "wave" (8 frames, plays ONCE and is also played REPEATEDLY while the viewer hovers): a friendly greeting to the viewer. He stays in profile facing right; feet, legs and sarong are fixed and identical in all 8 frames, no stepping and no drifting. This row is REPLAYED BACK TO BACK, so the step from frame 8 into frame 1 matters as much as every other step, and NO TWO NEIGHBOURING FRAMES MAY LOOK NEARLY IDENTICAL — a repeated frame reads as a stutter. Move the far arm by the SAME small amount every single frame. Frame 1: rest pose, exactly like idle frame 1, the far hand down at his side. Frame 2: the far hand has risen to hip height, elbow starting to bend. Frame 3: the hand is at shoulder height, palm beginning to turn toward the viewer. Frame 4: the hand is up beside the head, palm open toward the viewer, tilted to the LEFT; the head turns a little toward the viewer with a warm expression. Frame 5: the same raised hand tilted to the RIGHT. Frame 6: tilted to the LEFT again. Frame 7: the hand has come back down to shoulder height, palm turning inward. Frame 8: the hand is back at hip height — the SAME position as frame 2, and NOT the rest pose, so that the step from frame 8 into frame 1 is exactly as large as the step from frame 1 into frame 2. The hand keeps the same size, the same fingers and the same shape in every frame; only its position and angle change, rotating around the elbow and wrist. The tail gives one small happy flick on frames 4-5 and settles by frame 7.
