ROW "thanks" (8 frames, plays ONCE): he says THANK YOU to the viewer after being petted — a calm, dignified sembah, never a bow of servility and never a slapstick reaction. He stays in profile facing right; feet, legs and sarong fixed, no stepping and no drifting. Frame 1: rest pose equal to idle frame 1, arms relaxed. Frames 2-3: BOTH hands rise together toward the chest, elbows tucked in, moving in equal small steps. Frame 4: palms meet in front of the chest in a sembah, head beginning to dip, the single visible eye narrowing into a pleased crescent (no emoji face, no open smiling mouth). Frame 5: deepest point — head lowest, eye closed to a soft crescent, hands still joined at the chest. Frame 6: head rising back, hands beginning to open. Frame 7: palms open downward and outward at waist height, fingers relaxed, eye open again and warm. Frame 8: back to the rest pose (equal to frame 1) so the row leads into idle. Both hands ALWAYS move together and symmetrically, and they NEVER rise above the shoulders — the "wave" row is the one that lifts a single hand beside the head, and this row must be unmistakably different from it. The tail lifts in one slow gentle curve at most to hip height across frames 3-6 and settles by frame 8; it never curls into a question mark and never leaves the cell. No hearts, no sparkles, no speech bubble, no props.
